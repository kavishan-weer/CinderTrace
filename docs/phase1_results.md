# Phase 1 results (H3 resolution 6)

See [current coverage/area validation](coverage_validation.md) and [analytics contract](analytics_contract.md). The [H3 migration validation](h3_validation.md) for the square-grid comparison, integrity audit, and limitations.

Raw bounding-box files are preserved. Detections whose H3 cell belongs to the buffered region are kept and categorized; the web app filters by category.

| Tag | Values | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| `cat` | veg, volcano, static, offshore | Per H3 resolution-6 cell, so categories are disjoint and additive. Static = at least 10 labelled type-2 rows, at least 50% of the cell's labelled rows, in at least 3 years. Volcano = at least 3 type-1 rows, at least 50%. Cells with only NRT rows default to veg. NRT rows inherit their cell's category, which fills the catalog's missing `type` in recent data. |
| `conf` | 1 low, 2 nominal, 3 high | VIIRS l/n/h as published. MODIS 0-29 / 30-79 / 80-100 (FIRMS display convention; no official crosswalk exists). A cell-day takes the max confidence per sensor family. |
| `fam` | modis, terra, aqua, snpp, n20, n21 | modis = Terra+Aqua, deduplicated per cell-day |

Harmonization is fitted separately per region x cat x conf slice (fit on the 2012+ overlap, validated leave-one-year-out, monthly). Slices with too little overlap data fall back to a naive ratio and are flagged `low_data`.

## Validation, default filter (vegetation fires)

WAPE = weighted absolute percentage error (lower is better).

|---|---|---|---|
| Sri Lanka | low | 0.822 | 0.585 |
| Sri Lanka | nominal | 0.249 | 0.185 |
| Sri Lanka | high | 0.468 | 0.411 |
| California | low | 0.936 | 0.298 |
| California | nominal | 0.162 | 0.130 |
| California | high | 0.143 | 0.142 |
| New South Wales | low | 0.845 | 0.386 |
| New South Wales | nominal | 0.165 | 0.110 |
| New South Wales | high | 0.170 | 0.143 |

Low-confidence detections are the hardest to harmonize, which is why the app defaults to nominal and high.

## Outputs (`data/harmonized/`)
- `<region>_coverage.parquet`: reference-family monthly availability states and snapshot dates
- `<region>_sub_monthly.parquet`: selected-area monthly results, admin IDs/names, grid area and allocation/eligibility flags (generated by validation)
- `<region>_cells.parquet`: h3_cell, lon, lat, area_km2, sub, month, fam, cat, conf, cell_days, detections, frp (for the map and filters)
- `<region>_monthly.parquet`: month, cat, conf, modis_raw, viirs_snpp_raw, harmonized, lo, hi, source, model, low_data
- `<region>_report.json`: per-slice validation and seam tests

Reproduce: `python -m pipeline.fetch`, `python -m pipeline.boundaries`, then `python -m pipeline.harmonize`. Boundary building requires the source geoBoundaries files already present in `data/boundaries/`. Run `python -m pipeline.validate` to audit and compare against the preserved `data/baseline_square/harmonized/` snapshot.

Known limits: monthly-level validation only; Terra/Aqua orbit drift not modelled; static/volcano/offshore slices have little overlap data; H3 resolution 6 is used throughout. Region membership uses H3 centres inside a 0.03° buffered geoBoundaries outline, so clipping is approximate. Each cell is tagged by centre containment or nearest district/county/LGA (`sub`). Actual cell area is stored for normalization. Daily intermediate aggregates are not saved; monthly cells remain unharmonized, while regional monthly series are calibrated. Archive availability and selected-area transfer are implemented; see the analytics contract for conservative missing/provisional states and local-validation limits.
